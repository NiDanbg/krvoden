The Tree of Consciousness: An impossible model. An ancient secret. A conspiracy that threatens to reshape humanity.
Dr. Ethan Reed, a brilliant but work-obsessed neurobiologist, believes consciousness can be mapped. But when he discovers an anomaly in the brainwave data of Tibetan monks, he is confronted with the inexplicable. An enigmatic drawing, hidden in the depths of the human mind, which he calls the "Tree of Consciousness."
His investigation brings him face to face with Dr. Niya Sharma, an anthropologist who has found the same symbol carved in stone and depicted in manuscripts from twelve ancient, completely isolated civilizations. What Ethan believed to be a neurological anomaly turns out to be a lost map to the hidden potential of the human species – a key to unlocking higher states of consciousness.
But they are not the only ones searching for this key.
From the sterile labs of San Francisco to the snow-clad monasteries of the Himalayas, from the ancient libraries of Jerusalem to the impenetrable jungles of the Amazon – a desperate race against time begins everywhere.
The Tree of Consciousness is a breathtaking techno-thriller that weaves science with ancient mysteries. A story that explores the boundary between science and mysticism, freedom and control, and raises the terrifying possibility that the greatest threat to humanity comes not from without, but from within.
If you enjoy intelligent, fast-paced thrillers that make you think while you turn the pages, you will be immersed in this epic battle for the future of the human mind.
