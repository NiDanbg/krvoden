Dear friends and readers,
Following the amazing response to "SQUARE KNOT", I am absolutely thrilled to share the next long-awaited news with you!
The investigation is far from over. In just a matter of days, "IN THE GRIP OF SHADOWS" – the second book in the "BEYOND THE PETROHAN FILES" trilogy – will be released.
If the first book only lifted the curtain on unspoken truths, now we are diving even deeper. The stakes are higher, and the secrets are even darker. Expect the continuation of our Balkan noir, where shadows of the past come alive to settle scores with the present.
As before, you will soon be able to find the book on the shelves of the "Helikon" bookstores, as well as order it through the "Eurocom" TV shop.
Thank you from the bottom of my heart for your support of the first book! I can't wait to hear your thoughts on this new, even more thrilling adventure.
