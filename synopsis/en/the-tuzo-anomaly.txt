A FLAWED DIAGNOSIS COULD KILL THE PLANET.

An intelligent, relentlessly paced, and terrifyingly realistic techno-thriller in the finest traditions of the genre.

Global communications are dead. Satellites are falling from orbit. Lethal auroras blaze above the equator. Earth’s magnetic shield is collapsing, and unfiltered solar radiation threatens to scorch the surface.

As the world plunges into chaos, the U.S. military activates the Vulcan’s Hammer protocol. Their supercomputers have identified the enemy: a giant thermochemical anomaly in the mantle that is suffocating the Earth’s core. Uncompromising General Thomas Ryker plans to deploy thermonuclear charges deep inside the Ethiopian Danakil Depression to destroy the “tumor” and reboot the planet.

But Dr. Elina Ruud has deciphered Earth’s ancient past better than any computer. The rocks whisper a different truth to her. The anomaly isn’t killing the core—it’s protecting it. It is a pulsating pacemaker keeping the planet’s dynamo in motion.

The General’s strike won’t save humanity. It will stop the Earth’s heart forever.

Facing a military tribunal, lethal temperatures, and a violently unraveling geological catastrophe, Elina has only a few hours to prove she’s right before Ryker turns the detonation key. But the planet has no intention of waiting for humans to sort it out. Its immune system has already engaged... and we are the virus.

Why you'll love this book:

Hard Science Meets Explosive Action: Grounded in real geological phenomena (LLSVP structures, paleomagnetism, and the South Atlantic Anomaly), the novel fuses scientific fact with nail-biting suspense.

Claustrophobic Suspense: Set in the most inhospitable place on Earth, where searing temperatures and escalating tension push the human mind to its breaking point.

Characters on the Brink: A high-stakes clash between unyielding military logic and science’s desperate fight for survival.

If you crave scientifically grounded apocalyptic thrillers, disaster novels, and stories where human intellect faces off against the primal power of nature, The Tuzo Anomaly will keep you awake until the very last page.
