In the heart of the ice lies a secret that could save humanity... or erase it forever.

Deep within the ice of Svalbard, inside the Global Seed Vault, time has stood still. Until today.

Dr. Julian Hayes, a paleobotanist seeking redemption in the loneliest place on Earth, discovers an anomaly: an unregistered container concealing an ancient amphora. Inside it lies a seed that defies the laws of nature.

This is no mere relic. This is the Seed of Eden — the genetic key to immortality... or to absolute subjugation.

The discovery turns Julian into a target. Hunted by the mercenaries of a ruthless billionaire and a shadowy religious order, he has only one chance for survival. Julian must join forces with Alessandra Verdi, an expert in ancient codes, to unravel a secret hidden by Leonardo da Vinci himself 500 years ago.

From the Secret Archives of the Vatican to the castles of France and the windswept wilds of Patagonia, the two must decipher the genius's message before it is too late.

Because the choice isn't between life and death. The choice is between two tyrannies. And a third path exists — a symbiosis that no one has dared to attempt.

The Seed of Eden is an explosive techno-thriller where cutting-edge science meets ancient history in a battle for the future of our species.
