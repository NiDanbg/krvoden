An intelligent, relentlessly paced, and terrifyingly realistic techno-thriller in the finest traditions of the genre.

Global communications are dead. Satellites are falling from orbit. Lethal auroras blaze above the equator. Earth’s magnetic shield is collapsing, and unfiltered solar radiation threatens to scorch the surface.

As the world plunges into chaos, the U.S. military activates the Vulcan’s Hammer protocol. Their supercomputers have identified the enemy: a giant thermochemical anomaly in the mantle that is suffocating the Earth’s core.

But Dr. Elina Ruud has deciphered Earth’s ancient past better than any computer. The rocks whisper a different truth to her.
